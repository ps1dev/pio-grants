# PIO connector grants

Free connectors for the parallel I/O port on the back of the early PlayStation
models, for homebrew and development carts. The connector is the part that is hard
to find; if it is what keeps your cart off a real console, ask here.

The stock is split between the US and the UK. Requests are reviewed in one place
and sent from the stock nearest you.

The connector is free, and you pay the postage to get it to you.
[SHIPPING.md](SHIPPING.md) has the steps.

Grants go to projects that publish their design, so other people can build it
too. Cart designs come first. A link to a schematic, a repo or a work-in-progress
board makes the strongest request. Ask for the number the project needs.

To ask, open an issue with the [request form](../../issues/new?template=request.yml).
The reply is public, on the issue, yes or no with the reason.

Requests open once the stock is in hand.
